I'm Chirag — a student, and an active reader. This site is where I keep what I'm learning: notes from the books I'm reading, essays about writing and attention, and the occasional thing I build while I'm figuring things out.

Reading came first. I underline a lot, copy out the passages that stick, and then try to work out what I actually think about them — which is usually where a post comes from.

**What you'll find here**

- Essays about writing, reading and paying attention
- Notes on the tools and habits that survive a real week
- The occasional letter about whatever is on my desk
